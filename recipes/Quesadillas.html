Quesadillas recipe

Quesadillas are a popular Mexican dish made by filling tortillas with cheese and a variety of other ingredients like vegetables, beans, or meat, then grilling them until crispy and golden. They're quick to prepare and perfect for a casual lunch, dinner, or even a snack with friends.

Though simple, quesadillas deliver bold flavor and satisfying texture—with gooey melted cheese and a crunchy bite that make them a go-to comfort food.

Ingredients:

  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 1 cup cooked, shredded or chopped chicken (grilled or roasted)
  • 1 ½ cups shredded cheese (cheddar, mozzarella, or Mexican blend)
  • ½ red bell pepper, diced (optional)
  • ½ onion, finely sliced (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil or butter
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Salsa, guacamole, or sour cream for serving

Steps:

  1. Heat a bit of oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Sauté onion and bell pepper for 2-3 minutes, then add chicken, season, and remove from heat.
  3. In another pan, place one tortilla and sprinkle cheese over half of it.
  4. Add some of the chicken and vegetable mixture, then top with a little more cheese.
  5. Fold the tortilla in half and cook for 2-3 minutes on each side, until golden and the cheese is melted.
  6. Repeat with remaining tortillas.
  7. Slice into wedges and serve warm with salsa, guacamole, or sour cream.
