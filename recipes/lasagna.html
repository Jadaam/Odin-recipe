Lasagna recipes

Lasagna is a traditional italian dish made of layers of pasta, flavorful meat sauce, cremy béchamel, and melted cheese. Its a comforting meal; perfect for family dinners or gatherings with friends.

This recipe requires some time and patience, but the results is delicious: rich flavours and a tender,melt-in-your-mouth texture that make lasagna a classic homemade favorite.

Ingredients:

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground meat(beef)
  • Onion and garlic
  • Tomato sauce
  • béchamel sauce
  • Grated cheese
  • Salt and pepper
  • Herbs (basil,oregano)

Steps:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles as directed; drain and set aside.
  3. Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil, add ground beef, cook until browned, then stir in marinara sauce.
  4. Mix ricotta with egg, salt, and half the Parmesan.
  5. In a baking dish, layer: sauce, noodles, ricotta mix, mozzarella—repeat layers 2 more times.
  6. Top with remaining mozzarella and Parmesan.
  7. Cover with foil and bake 25 minutes; uncover and bake 20 more until bubbly.
  8. Let rest 10 minutes before serving.
